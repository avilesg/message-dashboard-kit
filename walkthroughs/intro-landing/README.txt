How to present: intro-landing-slides.html (7 slides)

1. Keep intro-landing-slides.html and personal-brain-architecture.jpg in the same folder.
   (The image on slide 7 uses a relative path.)
2. Open intro-landing-slides.html in Google Chrome (double-click, or drag it into a Chrome window).
3. Go fullscreen: press F11 (Windows/Linux), Ctrl+Cmd+F (Mac), or press F inside the deck.
4. In Zoom: Share Screen > pick the Chrome window (or the whole screen if you're in fullscreen).
5. Navigation:
   - Next: Right arrow, Down arrow, Space, Page Down, Enter, or left-click
   - Back: Left arrow, Up arrow, Page Up, Backspace, or right-click
   - Home / End jump to the first / last slide
   - Slide 7: click the architecture image to enlarge it, then click or press Esc to close it
   - The bottom-right corner shows "N / 7". You can also open a specific slide with #N at the end of the URL (e.g. ...html#6).
6. Works offline. The fonts fall back to the system sans-serif.
