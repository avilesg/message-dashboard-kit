01 · Sync friction

Your context doesn't travel with you.

Memory lives in each app, not in the model. Other apps only see it when you carry it over:

aShort handoffs

Paste a brief summary into the next tool.

bSecond opinion

Run the same prompt somewhere else.

cOwn the store

Notes, a graph, or a Mem0-class layer that you wire to each assistant.

Example: Perplexity
  • Notes and Brain live in Perplexity, the product. The model underneath can change, and the memory stays put.
  • Normal search uses one model: Best/auto, or the one you pick.
  • Model Council (Max) runs several models side by side, then a synthesizer shows where they agree, where they disagree, and what only one found.
  • You choose the lineup and shape the prompt. There's no public setting to weight models by topic.
  • Those model calls stay in Perplexity. They don't appear as conversations in your ChatGPT or Claude apps.
02 · Drift

Same question. Different answers.

  • Ask two assistants, or the same agent twice, and the answers can drift apart.
  • Nothing is broken. Each one fills the gaps with its own context.
  • So the real question is: which version do you trust?
"Summarize where our project stands."
Assistant A"On track. The vendor review is done."
Assistant B"Waiting on vendor sign-off before the next phase."
Same agent, next day"Two open risks: budget and timeline."
03 · Wording

The words you use shape what comes back.

  • Use non-standard wording and you can miss the details you needed.
  • Slang, team shorthand and industry terms often mean the same thing.
  • The context behind your words didn't come along.

04 · Curiosity

Everyone's talking about a "second brain."

A second brain, a digital twin. What would make it work across every AI you use?

Better notes?

More capture, more folders, more search. Useful, but every tool still reads your words its own way.

or

Shared meaning?

Agreed terms and how they connect, so each assistant starts from the same understanding.

Where Obsidian fits: durable Markdown you own, which you can connect through MCP, plugins or handoffs. It doesn't auto-merge with Perplexity Brain. The two work together when you bridge them on purpose. The best workflow, with ontology and governance on top, is still being researched.

05 · Bridge · pharma labs (VTI approach)

Pharma labs deal with this at global scale.

  • Labs get digitized, and systems spread across global orgs get integrated.
  • Processes run 10+ years, with different local vocabularies, and still need clean regulator submissions.
  • The focus is the meaning behind the words: preferred terms, their many alternative names, and how they relate. That's a knowledge graph.
