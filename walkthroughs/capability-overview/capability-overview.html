Your copy

  • Messages and media stay on your bot's computer
  • Nobody else's bot can see your dashboard

The shared recipe

  • Kit repo: README, tutorial, topics, scripts, prompts
  • Optional bot template: the same recipe, with guided setup
  • Contains no messages and no contacts

Shared vs private

What leaves your bot, and what never does

Private: stays on your bot's computer

  • The WhatsApp archive (messages + media)
  • The dashboard and its outputs
  • Group ID, names, people lists, alert keywords
  • personal.json (your private prefs backup, no messages)

Shared: the public recipe

  • Kit repo: tutorial, ontology, scripts, prompt templates
  • Optional bot template, scrubbed of personal data
  • A sample config, never your real one

Safety

Two rules that keep your group safe

🔒Send lock on

The bot reads the group; it never posts to it. Alerts and digests go only to your own bot chat.

⚠Don't republish

Group messages, names and photos are other people's content. Keep them in your private dashboard; don't share screenshots or exports.
